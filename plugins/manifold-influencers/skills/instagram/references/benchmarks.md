# Benchmarks and posting frequency

What "good" looks like on Instagram by format, by account size and by cadence, from the largest studies published in 2025 and 2026. Checked October 2026. Read the method before quoting any number: the base (followers or reach) changes which format wins.

## The base decides the winner

| Study | Sample | Base | Carousels | Reels | Images | All |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Socialinsider, 2026 report (2025 data) | 35M posts, 447,613 accounts | likes + comments over followers | 0.55% | 0.52% | 0.37% | 0.48%, down 24% on 2024 |
| Buffer, State of Social Media Engagement 2026 (2025 data) | Buffer users' posts | likes, comments, shares (saves where available) over reach | 6.90% | 3.31% | 4.44% | about 5.5%, down from about 7.3% in 2024 |

- Over followers, carousels and reels are nearly tied. Over reach, carousels earn about twice the engagement of reels, because reels are shown to many more people who do not react. Both are true; say which base a number uses, and never compare a rate over followers with one over reach.
- **Reels reach further:** 1.36 times the reach of carousels and 2.25 times that of single images (Buffer, 4M posts, January 2022 to October 2024). Socialinsider's 2025 data shows the opposite on its own "views" measure (carousels had more views per post than reels in every follower tier), but Instagram redefined views in August 2025 and the two studies count different things. Treat "reels for reach, carousels for depth" as the working rule and test it on the account's own numbers.
- **Engagement fell in 2025 on every large dataset:** Socialinsider down 24%, Buffer down 26%, Rival IQ down 16% (2025 report, 150 companies in each of 14 industries, rates over followers). A drop in an account's engagement rate over 2025 is partly the platform.
- **Industry changes the winning format.** Rival IQ's 2025 report: carousels beat reels overall and in alcohol, financial services, food and drink, nonprofits, higher education and tech; reels won in retail, home decor and travel; photos in health and beauty.

## By account size

Socialinsider's 2025 medians per post, by follower tier (it publishes no engagement rate by tier):

| Followers | Reel views | Carousel views | Saves per carousel | Comments per reel | Yearly follower growth |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1K to 5K | 580 | 993 | 1 | 3 | 22% |
| 10K to 50K | 2,460 | 4,275 | 8 | 12 | 17% |
| 100K to 1M | 16,035 | 35,370 | 98 | 60 | 11% |

- Follower growth roughly halved from 2024 to 2025 in every tier (38% to 22% for the smallest, 27% to 11% for 100K to 1M).
- **Followers predict reach poorly.** In Manifold's own pull of 15 accounts (see [field notes](field-notes.md)), brands' median reel reached about 6% of their follower count and creators' about 33%, with a 4K creator and a 152K creator getting similar views. Size a creator or a competitor on recent median views, never on followers.
- The engagement-rate-by-tier tables that circulate (nano, micro, macro) come from influencer-platform blogs without a published method; no large 2025 or 2026 study with a method reports one. Use the account's own median and its direct competitors.

## Posting frequency

- **More posts, more growth, with diminishing returns.** Buffer (August 2025; 2.1M posts from 102K accounts, comparing each account's weeks with its own baseline): weeks with 3 to 5 posts grew followers faster than weeks with 1 to 2, and 6 to 9 and 10 or more faster again; reach per post did not fall with more posts (about 12% higher at 3 to 5 a week, 24% at 10 or more, against 1 to 2). Correlational, and limited to Buffer users.
- **Instagram's line:** Mosseri (September 2026) says creators who post as much as they can raise their odds of a hit, and should pace themselves to avoid burning out. Instagram gives no number.
- **What accounts actually post:** brands average about 5 posts a week (Socialinsider, 2025); in 2025 they posted more reels (8 a month) and fewer single images (7, down from 10).
- **Timing:** no study with a method shows a best hour that holds across accounts. Buffer's best-time charts are a starting point for a test, not a rule. Recency matters (the feed runs roughly chronologically among ranked posts), so post when the audience is awake, in its time zone.

## Openings and sound

- **Speech in the first seconds helps retention.** Emplifi (10,110 Facebook Reels from 704 brand pages, March 2026): speech in the first three seconds lifted 10-second retention by almost 25% against music alone, and a person on screen in the first three seconds by about 10%, an advantage that faded by 30 seconds. Facebook data; untested on Instagram, but the same Meta Reels system.
- Meta (December 2025) says Reels ads with music or voiceover deliver up to 13% more incremental conversions; that is an ads figure.

## What nobody has measured

- How much a send is worth against a like or a save, in numbers. Instagram ranks them but publishes no weights; the "sends are worth 3 to 5 likes" claims have no source.
- Engagement by follower tier with a published method, for 2025 or 2026.
- Whether a Trial Reel that does well and is shared to followers performs like a normal post afterwards.
