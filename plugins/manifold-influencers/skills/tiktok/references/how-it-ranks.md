# How TikTok ranks videos

What TikTok has actually documented about For You and search, what the 2026 US ownership change did and did not change, and where the evidence runs out. Read it before telling anyone how "the algorithm" works: most of what circulates has no source.

## What TikTok documents

TikTok's fullest public account of For You is still its newsroom post of 18 June 2020. Its support pages have since been reworded but add no weights or thresholds.

- **Three groups of signals.** User interactions (videos liked or shared, comments, accounts followed, videos created), video information (captions, sounds, hashtags), and device and account settings (language, country, device type), which TikTok says carry less weight than the other two because users do not choose them as preferences.
- **Strong beats weak.** Finishing a longer video from start to end is named as a strong signal; viewer and creator being in the same country as a weak one. This is the only documented basis for "watch time matters". No percentage threshold is documented: the "70% completion" rule that SEO blogs repeat has no TikTok source.
- **Followers are not a direct factor.** TikTok says neither follower count nor a history of high-performing videos is a direct factor, while noting a bigger account gets more views because more people follow it. Judge progress on median views, not followers.
- **Diversity.** For You generally avoids two videos in a row with the same sound or from the same creator, so one account cannot flood a feed.
- **Eligibility.** TikTok's Community Guidelines keep a separate set of standards for content that may stay up but is not recommended in For You, unoriginal reposts among it. A video that gets almost no views beyond followers may be ineligible rather than unlucky; TikTok Studio shows the share of views from For You.

What it does not say: how the first audience for a new video is picked, how long a video stays in testing, or how search ranking differs from For You. Treat any claim about "batches of 300 viewers" or "the first hour" as folklore unless it cites TikTok.

## What the numbers say about reach

- **Organic reach per follower has been falling since 2022.** Across 780,000 videos from 1,000 profiles, the share of followers a typical post reached fell from up to 24% in early 2022 to about 10% by the end of 2023; 85% of accounts lost reach (Fanpage Karma, 2024). More creators competing for the same attention is the likelier cause than a single change.
- **2025 split small accounts from big ones.** Average views per post, 2024 to 2025 (Socialinsider, 2M videos, 214,507 profiles): 1K to 5K followers 860 to 350; 5K to 10K 1,575 to 945; 10K to 50K 3,655 to 3,240; 50K to 100K 8,688 to 9,900; 100K to 1M 25,198 to 34,900. Posting frequency rose in every tier, by roughly a third to three quarters. Follower growth fell in every tier. The report's own headline percentages do not match its tables; quote the tables.
- **Views can come from far outside the follower base.** In our meal-prep sample, 22 of the 30 most liked videos of the month had more views than their account had followers ([field notes](field-notes.md)). Views above followers mean For You carried the video; the cause is in the video.

## TikTok search

- **Who uses it.** 49% of US consumers had used TikTok as a search engine, up from 41% in 2024; 65% of Gen Z, 55% of millennials, 40% of Gen X, 12% of boomers. Yet only 25% of Gen Z found it effective, and the share of Gen Z who would rely on TikTok over Google fell from 8% to 4%; 85% named Google the most helpful (Adobe Express, 807 US consumers and 200 small business owners, January 2026). A TikTok-commissioned study (WARC, September 2025, about 1,000 US consumers) found 86% of Gen Z search TikTok weekly against 90% for Google; TikTok sells search ads, so weigh it accordingly.
- **What people search for there.** Video tutorials (61%), product reviews (45%), personal stories (41%), creator recommendations (33%) (Adobe, 2026). Fashion and beauty, cooking, entertainment and DIY lead in TikTok's own study. A B2B category term is usually thin: our "crm software" search for the month had a median of about 1,900 views across its top 30, in six languages ([field notes](field-notes.md)).
- **What it reads.** TikTok documents captions, sounds and hashtags. That it also indexes speech and on-screen text is claimed by every SEO vendor and is plausible, but no TikTok page states weights. The safe practice: say the search phrase out loud early, put it on screen, and write it naturally in the caption, which can run to thousands of characters; many creators now paste the transcript into the caption.
- **Creator Search Insights.** TikTok Studio shows creators what people search for in their topics, including topics where searches outrun the videos. Creators in our samples tag videos made from it (#creatorsearchinsights), so it is an active TikTok programme. It is the closest thing to keyword data TikTok offers; the tools cannot see it.
- **Searches inside Manifold** are TikTok's ranked search, not a keyword volume. Never present result counts or views as search demand.

## The US joint venture

- **What happened.** TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC was announced on 22 to 23 January 2026 under the September 2025 executive order. Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX hold 15% each, ByteDance 19.9%, other investors the rest. The venture controls US data (stored in Oracle's US cloud), trust and safety and content moderation; Oracle reviews the source code. The recommendation algorithm is licensed from ByteDance and is to be retrained, tested and updated on US user data. ByteDance keeps running US advertising and e-commerce.
- **What changed for marketers.** Ad accounts, Ads Manager, TikTok Shop and creator payouts carried on without migration. New terms and a privacy policy pop-up in January drew a spike in uninstalls (daily US uninstalls up nearly 150% over five days, about 2.5 times the previous three months' average; Sensor Tower to CNBC, 26 January 2026), but no mass exodus followed (CNBC, February 2026). Outages in late January and March 2026 broke uploads and view counts for hours.
- **What is not known.** As of October 2026 no public statement says the retraining is finished or what it changed. Reports of lower reach and payouts since are creator anecdotes and a self-reported agency survey, confounded by the outages. When a user's views moved in 2026, compare against their own pre-2026 median and against competitors over the same weeks before blaming the owners.
- **Outside the US** nothing changed: other markets run on ByteDance's system as before. A US-only reach shift does not generalise.

## Controls viewers now have

- **AI content.** Since 19 November 2025 the Manage Topics settings include a slider to see less AI-generated content (it cannot be turned off entirely). TikTok detects AI media through C2PA content credentials and its own invisible watermarks, and labels it; creators must label realistic AI content themselves.
- **Topic sliders and "Not interested"** let viewers turn whole categories down. A feed is built from the viewer's choices as much as from the video.

## TikTok Shop and reach

No independent study shows that a product link raises or lowers a video's organic reach; vendor blogs claim both, without data. TikTok's seller materials treat product links as a normal feature. Hard-sell openings (a product callout in the first seconds, a scripted testimonial) plausibly lose viewers whatever the link. If the user needs an answer, have them compare their own linked and unlinked videos on the same topic, length and posting time, in TikTok Studio, over at least ten of each. Shop sales, GMV and affiliate data are not in the tools.
