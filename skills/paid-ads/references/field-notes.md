# Field notes

What Manifold has seen in its own runs of the ad library and search tools. Small samples: read them as evidence of what the fields contain and where they mislead, not as market shares. Newest first.

## One category across four libraries (15 October 2026)

Inputs: "crm software" with `ads_search_ads` on Meta, TikTok and LinkedIn (one page each, no `country`, `active_only` off); `ads_get_advertiser_ads` on Google for pipedrive.com with `country: "US"` (one page); `ads_get_ad` on two ads per library; `seo_get_serp` on "pipedrive" (desktop and mobile), "hubspot pricing", "crm software" and "car insurance quotes" (desktop); `seo_get_keyword_metrics` on six brand and category terms. 23 credits.

**Meta (29 rows).** 12 running, 17 stopped. The stopped ones were mostly ads shown in the EU, which Meta keeps for a year; one German advertiser's ad had run from January 2024 to February 2026. Five rows were off-topic (remote-job and virtual-assistant ads matching "crm"). Two of the 12 running ads had a `last_shown` weeks old while `active` said running. On the EU ad, `ads_get_ad` returned reach (1,989,155), reach by age and gender, targeting (Germany, 18 to 65, all genders) and the payer; its country reach (2,170,823) did not match the total. A US ad from the same search returned no reach, targeting or countries.

**TikTok (12 rows).** Every advertiser was European or British (Romanian, Hungarian and UK companies, a Spanish-language creator). Every ad ran between 17 and 25 September 2026, none later, though the run was on 15 October: the library lags about three weeks. Run lengths were 0 to 6 days. `ads_get_ad` on two ads returned no `body` (the list rows had one), no countries and no objective; one returned a `destination_url`.

**LinkedIn (24 rows).** 14 rows had dates and an impressions range, and every one of those first ran in the three days before the run: keyword search returns the newest ads first. 10 rows had no dates or impressions. Five were individuals' posts promoted as ads. `countries` carried retired codes (DD where the targeting named Berlin, RH, CS, VD, YD) and once a raw name ("Palestinian Territories"); `reach_by_country` carried the same codes. LinkedIn sends country names, and the tool's name-to-code table picked the retired code sharing each name; it now gives the current code (DE, ZW, RS, VN, YE) and PS. `targeting` read correctly.

**Google (39 rows for pipedrive.com).** 34 were Pipedrive Inc.'s own, 4 an agency's display ads, 1 an unrelated German digital-signage company. All 34 of Pipedrive's were still running; 22 had run 90 days or more, 3 for 30 to 89, and 9 under 30. Rows came sorted by `last_shown`, not age. `ads_get_ad` returned the text ("Pipedrive Pricing", a brand-term search ad) and countries but **no `first_shown`**, though the list row had one: Google's ad record leaves its own date empty. The tool now falls back to the earliest region's date, which this US-only ad did not carry either, so its date is still the list row's. The unrelated advertiser's EU-shown ad carried an impressions range ("150000 to 175000") and first and last dates per country; Pipedrive's US-only ad carried neither.

**Search result pages.** **No `type: "paid"` row in any of six fetches**, including "car insurance quotes" (top-of-page bids of $53 to $151) and "crm software". A seventh fetch of "car insurance quotes", straight from DataForSEO the same day, had none in the raw response either: the provider's crawl is rarely served ads. `seo_get_serp` cannot show who bids on a term, and its description now says so. The "pipedrive" mobile fetch showed no pipedrive.com result in its top organic rows, which looks like the fetch rather than Google.

**Keyword prices.** `cpc` and the top-of-page range disagree: "hubspot alternative" $118.68 `cpc` against a $12.21 to $63.63 range; "crm software" $37.13 with `competition` 0.04. "pipedrive" (49,500 searches) $11.35, with a range up to $58.55, so the bare brand name is a contested auction.
