# How each platform buys now

What changed on Meta, Google, LinkedIn and TikTok in 2025 and 2026, and what it changes in a plan or a brief. Prices are in [benchmarks](benchmarks.md); ads inside ChatGPT and Google's AI answers are in [AI answer ads](ai-answer-ads.md). Checked October 2026; these platforms change monthly, so re-check anything older than six months against the platform's own help pages.

## Meta: the creative is the targeting

- **Andromeda** is Meta's ad retrieval system, the first stage of delivery that cuts tens of millions of eligible ads to a few thousand candidates before ranking. Meta's engineering post (December 2024) reports +6% recall and +8% ads quality on selected segments, a 10,000x increase in model capacity, and says it was built because Advantage+ and generative AI multiply the number of ads to choose from. Meta has been rolling it out since late 2024.
- **What it changes:** retrieval matches each ad to the people its content suits, so the set of creatives decides who an ad set reaches. Meta's one official recommendation tied to Andromeda is creative diversification: ads that differ in format, visual and message, with primary text that differs significantly rather than subtly (Meta, as reported by Jon Loomer, October 2025). Ten crops of one image are one creative to the system.
- **In practice:** brief several distinct concepts (a different person, problem, setting or format), not variants of one. Many near-identical ads compete for the same retrieval slot and teach nothing. More ads are not automatically better; Meta has given no number.
- **Advantage+** (automated audiences, placements and creative) is the default buying mode. Meta reports a 22% ROAS increase for advertisers turning on Advantage+ targeting for the first time (Meta, December 2024; Meta's own figure). Broad audiences plus strong creative now beat narrow interest stacks for most advertisers; narrow targeting mainly cuts the pool retrieval can learn from.
- **Learning phase:** Meta's guidance is about 50 optimisation events a week per ad set to leave learning. Weekly budget divided by the allowable cost per result under 50 means optimising for an earlier event (lead, trial start, add to cart), or fewer ad sets.
- **Not settled:** whether "creative similarity" is penalised as such, how many concepts an ad set should carry, and how much Advantage+ creative enhancements help. Vendors claim numbers; none has published a method.

## Google: fewer keywords, more automation, more controls

- **Performance Max got its controls in 2025.** Campaign-level negative keywords (from January 2025, up to 10,000 per campaign from March 2025), shared negative lists, search terms reporting with a column saying whether a query came from a search theme or keywordless matching, 50 search themes per asset group (was 25), age and device exclusions, full asset reporting, and a channel performance report (announced April 2025). PMax is no longer a black box; an account that has not added negatives and read the search terms since 2025 is leaving money there.
- **AI Max for Search** layers query expansion, generated copy and final-URL expansion onto a Search campaign. Google claims 14% more conversions at similar CPA, 27% for campaigns mostly on exact and phrase match (Google, 2025, its own data). An independent read of 250+ AI Max campaigns found a median +13% conversion value but +16% CPA, ROAS from +42% to -35%, and only 22% of campaigns landing near target (Smarter Ecommerce, March 2026). Treat it as an expansion layer on a sound keyword core, with its own CPA expectation, and watch its search terms.
- **Dynamic Search Ads upgrade automatically to AI Max from September 2026** (Google, April 2026). An account still on DSA has already changed behaviour; check its search terms.
- **AI Mode and AI Overviews take ads only from AI Max for Search or Performance Max** with text customisation on, plus Shopping (see [AI answer ads](ai-answer-ads.md)). An exact-match-only account cannot appear there.
- **Overlap splits learning.** In the same Smarter Ecommerce sample, about half the accounts ran AI Max, DSA and PMax together, competing for the same queries. One query, one campaign where possible.

## LinkedIn: expensive clicks, long journeys, real B2B return

- Clicks cost several times Meta's (see [benchmarks](benchmarks.md)), and the journey is long: 272 days from first touch to revenue in Dreamdata's 2026 report, up from 211. A LinkedIn test judged on 30-day pipeline will look like a loss.
- In the same report LinkedIn returned the most revenue per ad dollar of any B2B channel (121% company-level ROAS against 67% for Google Search and 51% for Meta), and took 41% of B2B paid social budgets. Dreamdata sells B2B attribution and the data is its own customers': a vendor figure on its own model, but the largest B2B journey dataset published.
- **Thought Leader ads** (an employee's or founder's post promoted as an ad) were about one row in five in our LinkedIn library sample. They carry a person's voice into the feed and are worth testing beside company ads.
- LinkedIn earns its price only when a deal is worth enough to carry clicks at several times Meta's cost, and the buyer can be named by title, company size or industry.

## TikTok: cheap reach, weaker conversion

- Impressions are the cheapest of the four (median CPM about $4 against about $15 on Meta, Triple Whale, 2025 to 2026), but conversion rate and CTR fell over the year while CPM fell faster. TikTok buys attention cheaply; it converts less of it.
- Creative that looks like a native post (a person talking to camera, captions, the product or problem in the first seconds) is the format the platform rewards. Spark Ads promote an existing organic or creator post, which carries its comments and social proof; the creator side belongs to [creators](../../creators/SKILL.md).
- The TikTok ad library covers Europe only (see [ad libraries](ad-libraries.md#tiktok)), so a US competitor's TikTok ads are invisible to the tools.

## Choosing between them

- **Search first** when buyers already search for the solution: real volume on commercial and transactional terms means demand to capture before demand to create.
- **Meta or TikTok first** when the product has to be shown, the price is impulse-level, or nobody searches for the category yet.
- **LinkedIn** when the deal value is high and the buyer is nameable by job; never as the only test on a small budget.
- **One platform while spend is under about $5,000 a month** (a rule of thumb): a budget split across three gives none of them enough events to learn on.
