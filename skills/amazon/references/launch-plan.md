# Launch plan

A 30-60-90 day plan for a new product on Amazon, or a relaunch of one that never took off. The order follows what blocks what: a listing that breaks the rules or a product that cannot win the Buy Box wastes every ad dollar sent to it.

## Before day 1: is it worth launching?

- **Demand and competition:** the core terms and their volumes ([keyword research](keyword-research.md)), the page-one price band, ratings and `bought_past_month` floors on those terms, and how many page-one products carry fewer than a few hundred ratings. A page where every organic listing has thousands of ratings at 4.5 stars is a long climb; one with several young listings selling 1K+ a month is open.
- **Economics:** contribution per unit after referral, FBA, surcharge, storage and landed cost ([fees](fees.md)), and the break-even ACoS that leaves. If break-even ACoS is below the category's typical ACoS ([ads](ads.md)), the launch needs a higher price, a lower cost or an off-Amazon traffic source.
- **Brand Registry** if the user owns the brand: it unlocks A+ content, Sponsored Brands, Vine, Brand Analytics and the Brand Referral Bonus.
- Baseline cost with Manifold: about 95 credits for keywords and demand, about 35 for a rival-based listing audit, about 20 for a sellers and price check.

## The 90 days

| Period | Work | KPI to re-measure |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1 to 30 | Listing built to the 2026 rules: a 75-character title, Item Highlights, bullets that answer buyer questions, every attribute filled, backend terms under the byte limit, a compliant main image and a full image set ([listing audit](listing-audit.md)). Stock deep enough to avoid low-inventory fees and stock-outs. Vine enrolled. Sponsored Products on the core and long-tail terms, with an auto campaign to harvest search terms. | Listing live with no suppressed fields; first ratings in; the ASIN appears on its core terms in `amazon_search_products` (the keyword index lags about two weeks) |
| Days 31 to 60 | Search-term report pruned: negatives on terms that spend without selling, budget to the terms that convert. Bullets and images revised against the first reviews' complaints. Off-Amazon traffic (email, creators, social) through Amazon Attribution links for the Brand Referral Bonus. Sponsored Brands once the listing converts. | Organic position on core terms against day 30; ACoS trend against break-even; rating at or above 4.0 |
| Days 61 to 90 | Ads shifted from discovery to defence on the terms where the listing now ranks; price tested in small steps with the Buy Box watched ([pricing and the Buy Box](buy-box.md)); a check of what the assistant recommends for the buyer's questions ([search ranking](search-ranking.md)). | Organic share of sales (from the user's reports); TACoS falling; Buy Box held; named by the assistant on at least some buyer questions |

Give each period an owner and three first actions for this week. Time big changes away from Prime Day and the Q4 peak: change a title or price in the weeks before, not the days before.

## Setting targets honestly

- Promise inputs and leading signals: a compliant, complete listing; reviews through permitted channels; rank on named terms. No one can promise a page-one position or a recommendation from the assistant.
- Organic rank on a competitive term can take months and depends on sales the ads help create. Say that the length of any "honeymoon" for new listings has never been measured.
- Organic share of sales and TACoS come from the user's Seller Central and ads console; Manifold cannot measure them. Ask for them at days 30, 60 and 90.
- Re-measure ranks and pages with the same terms each time; the host stores the dates, terms, positions and credits spent. Manifold keeps no history.

## Never

Buy reviews, run review clubs or rebate schemes, ask only happy buyers to review, or create a second account to list the same product. Each risks the account and, for reviews, FTC penalties ([reviews](reviews.md)).
