# A creator program plan

When the user wants a plan rather than a list: where the niche's creators are, what competitors already get from creators, which deals fit the goal and budget, over 90 days. It draws on the rest of this skill for the work itself.

## Settle first

Ask in one message; use the default and say so for anything unanswered.

- **Goal:** awareness, sales through codes and links, content for ads, or buzz for a moment. Default: sales through codes, because the user can count them.
- **Stage:** pre-launch, early or established, and whether they have worked with creators. Default: early, none yet.
- **Budget:** cash for fees, product for gifting, credits for research. Default: $3,000 and 30 units for the quarter, about 500 credits.
- **Team:** who manages creators, approves drafts, ships product. Default: the founder, a few hours a week.
- **Competitors:** two or three; default the brands that show up in the niche's sponsored posts.

## Baseline

About 40 credits. Per platform, note how many distinct creators post and their median views.

- **Where the niche lives:** the niche keyword on TikTok and YouTube (`since: "year"` on YouTube) and the niche hashtag on Instagram, two pages each.
- **What competitors get:** the same searches with each competitor's name; keep sponsored posts. `ads_get_advertiser_ads` on each competitor on Meta and TikTok: a creator's video in a competitor's ads means the competitor bought usage rights.
- **Re-hires:** one page of recent posts on five creators competitors paid. A competitor paying the same creator twice is the best public sign a deal paid off; a creator's video still running as an ad a month after `first_shown` is the other.
- **Deal types in use:** codes in captions mean affiliate deals, "gifted" means seeding, a sponsored signal without a code means a flat fee, a creator video in the ad library means bought rights.
- **The user's footprint:** the brand name in the same searches. Unpaid fans are the cheapest first gifting list ([fans and affiliates](fans-and-affiliates.md)).
- **The opening:** the platform with many niche creators and few competitors present. The one where every competitor is present is table stakes.

## The 90 days

| Period | Work | KPI the tools can re-measure |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1 to 30 | Shortlist of 30 ([finding](finding.md)), top 10 vetted ([vetting](vetting.md)), product to 15 to 20 nano and micro creators, the brief written ([brief](brief.md)) | Gifted creators who posted; their posts' views against their own median |
| Days 31 to 60 | Three to five paid posts from vetted micro and mid creators, each with its own code | Each post's views against the creator's median; buying questions in its comments; code redemptions from the user's store |
| Days 61 to 90 | Re-hire the two whose posts beat their median; run the best posts as ads ([paid-ads](../../paid-ads/SKILL.md)); drop the rest | Cost per redemption per creator; count of posts naming the brand in the baseline searches |

Give each period an owner and three first actions for this week. Codes and sales live in the user's store: ask for them at each checkpoint. The server keeps no state, so the host keeps the baseline; weekly brand-mention reruns belong to [brand-monitoring](../../brand-monitoring/SKILL.md).

## Setting targets honestly

- Gifted creators choose whether and when to post; plan gifted posts as a bonus, paid ones as commitments.
- A creator's next sponsored post will most likely land under their organic median (the usual case in our [field notes](field-notes.md)). Forecast reach at half the median, not the median.
- Codes undercount: a share of buyers see the post and buy later without the code. Say so beside every cost-per-sale figure; no study measured how much.

## Creators for a launch

- **Count:** about 15 small creators (10K to 100K followers) rather than one large one, unless the date needs a guaranteed audience.
- **Launch fit:** one page of recent posts per creator. Do they cover new products ("I tried", unboxings), take paid posts, and post often enough to hit a date? A creator who has never shown a product will not start with the user's.
- **Dates:** product in hand by T-21 (T-30 for YouTube, where a review takes longer), brief by T-10 with the embargo, live between launch day and T+3, a code per creator. Vet paid creators the launch leans on; skip vetting for gifted posts to small creators.
- The rest of the launch (channels, timing, the day itself) belongs to [growth-strategy](../../growth-strategy/SKILL.md).
