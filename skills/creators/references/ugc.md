# UGC creators

UGC creators film videos the brand posts on its own accounts or runs as ads: reviews, unboxings, demos, problem and solution, "TikTok made me buy it". The user pays per video and for the rights, so the creator's audience matters little and the craft matters most. UGC was the fastest-growing campaign type on Collabstr's marketplace in 2025 (up 133%, 35% of campaigns), so good small makers are busier than a year ago.

## Settle first

- **Product and proof of fit:** two or three competitor or adjacent products whose reviews count as evidence.
- **Styles:** review and unboxing by default; add demo, before and after, get ready with me as the product needs.
- **On camera:** a face suits trust-heavy categories (skincare, supplements, finance); hands and voice-over suit products that do the talking. Ask before cutting the list.
- **Size:** 1K to 50K followers by default. Small creators charge less, answer faster and look like customers, which is the point.
- **Rights:** organic, paid ads, product page; from the brand's handle or the creator's; for how long. They set the price ([rates](rates.md#running-posts-from-the-creators-handle)).

## Where makers show up

- **TikTok:** `tiktok_search_videos` pairing a style with the category ("<category> review", "<competitor> honest review", "tiktok made me buy it <category>", "<product type> unboxing"), two pages each, `since: "year"` in a small category.
- **Instagram:** hashtags such as ugccreator, ugc plus the category, the category plus review.
- **YouTube:** "<category> review" when the category has long-form reviewers.
- **Ad libraries:** `ads_search_ads` with the category on `platform: "tiktok"` and `platform: "facebook"`. A person talking to camera is UGC a brand bought; when it runs from a creator's handle or names one, that creator is a proven maker.

Keep authors with two or more product-style videos, or one strong one (a clear demo with a verdict). Drop brand accounts, resellers and pages reposting other people's videos. Bios that say "UGC creator", list a portfolio or rate card, or a business email mark a working maker.

## Judging craft

- **Hear them.** `tiktok_get_transcript` or `instagram_get_transcript` on one product video each for the top ten (1 credit; Instagram covers reels up to two minutes). A maker who opens with a hook, names the problem, shows the product working and ends with a verdict can make an ad. One who talks for twenty seconds before the product appears cannot.
- **Length:** `duration_s` of 15 to 60 seconds is ad length.
- **Views on their own posts are a weak signal here.** A 3K-follower creator with tight 30-second reviews beats a 200K creator who rambles.
- **Buying questions** in the comments of their own "made me buy it" video show they can sell. Mark those rows.
- **Paid brand work in the feed is a plus,** not a warning: it shows they deliver to brands.

## Price and rights

- Manifold's working rule of thumb for the US in 2026 (not a measured benchmark): about $150 to $500 for a video without posting on the creator's account; paid usage beyond 30 days, raw footage and extra hooks cost more.
- The tools cannot see rights. The brief names every use, the duration and whose handle the ad runs from ([brief](brief.md)). Running from the creator's handle needs a Spark Ads code or partnership-ads permission.
- A UGC video the brand posts as if from a customer still needs the connection disclosed when it carries the creator's face or voice as a testimonial, and the FTC's reviews rule bans presenting it as an independent customer review ([disclosure](disclosure.md)).

## Cost and delivery

About 78 credits for 20 makers: five TikTok queries and three Instagram hashtags at two pages, two ad-library searches, 30 profiles, 20 listings, 10 transcripts. Deliver: creator, platform and link, followers, styles, best example (URL, views), its hook from the transcript, on camera (face, voice-over, hands), brand-work signs, portfolio or contact, and a line on fit. Sort by craft. The ad angles the maker works from belong to [paid-ads](../../paid-ads/SKILL.md).
