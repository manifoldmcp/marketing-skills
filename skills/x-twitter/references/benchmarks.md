# Benchmarks and audience

What "good" looks like on X, and who is there. Checked October 2026. The single most important fact: published X engagement rates differ by up to a hundred times because each vendor divides by something different. Quote a benchmark only with its formula, and prefer the account's own median.

## Engagement rate: four formulas, four answers

| Source | Formula | X median | Sample |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rival IQ (now Quid), 2026 report | all interactions over followers, per post | 0.03% (doubled from 0.015%) | 150 companies per industry, random |
| Socialinsider, 2026 benchmarks | likes and reposts over followers | 0.12% for 2025 (0.15% in 2024) | brands, 70M posts across four networks; no X-only count given |
| Buffer, Premium study (Oct 2025) | likes, replies, reposts; denominator not stated | about 0.5% paid tiers, 0% free | 18.8M posts, 71K accounts |
| Buffer, State of Engagement (Mar 2026) | likes, reposts, comments over reach or impressions | about 2.5% to 2.8% for 2025 | 52M posts across ten networks |

- Per-follower rates (Rival IQ, Socialinsider) punish large accounts; per-impression rates (Buffer) do not. A brand at the Rival IQ median with 10,000 followers gets three interactions a post.
- Buffer's own author attributes its 44% rise in X's 2025 median to changes in who uses X or in metric definitions, not better performance.
- By industry (Rival IQ): sports, automotive, aviation and influencer accounts lead on X; beauty, retail and media trail, media despite posting the most.
- Socialinsider's brand sample: posting rose from 50 to 70 a month in 2025 while median likes per post fell from 40 to 15 and replies and reposts stayed at 1. Brands post more on X for less.

For an audit use per-view numbers from `views`, which every vendor can match: our October 2026 reads ranged from 0.16% (TechCrunch, all link posts) to 1.19% (Stripe) median engagement per view ([field notes](field-notes.md)).

## Reach

- **Free accounts have very little.** Buffer: median under 100 impressions per post for non-subscribers, about 600 for Premium, over 1,550 for Premium+; overall median reach fell from about 1,000 (August 2024) to under 750 (August 2025). OpenTweet's mostly small accounts: a median post of about 50 impressions (2026).
- **Views per follower** is the comparable reach number across account sizes. Our reads: Vercel 0.22, Stripe 0.11, Coinbase 0.017, TechCrunch 0.0014. A large account below 0.01 has an audience that stopped looking, or posts so often that each post competes with the last.
- Socialinsider's brand median rose to about 2,979 views a post in 2025 (from 1,430), while likes fell: more views, less interaction per view.

## Who is on X

- **Pew, 2025** (5,022 US adults, February to June 2025): X used by 25% of men and 16% of women; 33% of 18 to 29 year olds, 25% of 30 to 49, 16% of 50 to 64, 10% of 65 and over; 24% of college graduates against 16% with high school or less; 25% to 26% in households over $70,000. 10% of US adults use it daily, fewer than WhatsApp's 32% use overall.
- **The partisan flip:** 24% of Republicans and 19% of Democrats use X; two years earlier it was 20% against 26%. Assume a US audience on X now leans right of the population, which matters for consumer brands and for any political or culture-war topic.
- **News:** 12% of US adults regularly get news on X, and 57% of X users say they do (Pew, August 2025). More men (17%) than women (8%), and more 18 to 29 year olds (21%).
- **Where X still matters:** tech and AI builders, developers, founders and investors, crypto, finance and markets, politics, sports, and live news. These are observations shared by every source above rather than a measured market share; no 2025-2026 study sizes X's tech or crypto audience.
- **Unknown:** reliable daily active user counts. X's own claims and third-party traffic panels disagree and none was verified for this skill; do not quote one.
