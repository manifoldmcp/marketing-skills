# X strategy

Whether X is worth the effort for this business, whose account should carry it, and what to post given how X ranks in 2026. Checked October 2026.

## Is X the right channel

- **Yes, usually,** when the buyers are developers, AI and tech builders, founders, investors, crypto or finance people, journalists, or a politically engaged audience, and when news about the category breaks there first ([benchmarks](benchmarks.md#who-is-on-x)).
- **Weak** for a mainstream consumer audience, women over 30, or buyers over 50: Pew's 2025 numbers make X a minority platform for them, and its US audience now leans right.
- **Never the only channel** for a brand starting from zero: free accounts' median post reaches under 100 people (Buffer, 2025). Start where a small account gets distribution, or bring an audience.
- Check before committing: audit two competitors and two people the buyers follow ([audit](audit.md)). If none draws more than 0.01 views per follower, the category is quiet on X.

## Whose account

- **The founder or an expert on the team usually beats the company account** for the same news: people reply to people, and replies, quotes and DM shares are what the ranking weights ([algorithm](algorithm.md#the-weights)). The company account announces; the person discusses.
- The mutual-follow reply boost (July 2026) rewards an account that follows back and talks with its peers. A founder who follows and replies to the niche's builders gets ranked higher for them.
- A company account still earns its keep for launches, changelogs, customer stories and support redirects: Vercel's link-heavy feed drew 0.22 views per follower in October 2026 ([field notes](field-notes.md)).

## What to post

From the weights and the 2025-2026 studies, not folklore:

- **Write for replies and private shares.** A specific claim, a number, a question the niche argues about, a teardown or a how-to people send to a colleague. A post people only like barely moves.
- **Text is a strong default on X.** It led Buffer's 2026 format medians on X, the one major network where it does; add an image when it carries information, video when the thing moves.
- **Links: in the post, with a post that stands alone.** X says it does not penalise links and the published code has no link penalty, but a bare link draws nothing. Measure link and no-link posts on the account before changing habits ([algorithm](algorithm.md#links)).
- **Threads:** only the opener is ranked for most viewers; put the whole value proposition in it. A long single post (Premium allows it) or an X Article is the alternative.
- **Reply to others' posts** where the buyers are. Replies are discounted in For You and dropped for non-followers, so their value is the thread itself and the profile visits, not reach.
- **Avoid what triggers negatives:** a report weighs −234 and a mute −58.8 on predicted probability. Engagement bait, reply-guy spam and off-topic provocation lose reach with the people who matter even when they win replies from others.
- **48 hours.** A post's ranked life is two days; spacing posts so each gets its window beats bursts.

## Premium

- For a business or founder whose buyers are on X: subscribe (Premium, or Verified Organizations for a company), then judge after a month by views per follower against the pre-Premium median. Buffer's measured gap is about ten times the median reach of free accounts, but it is an association and the published For You score has no Premium term ([algorithm](algorithm.md#premium-and-the-checkmark)).
- A checkmark is not trust: anyone can buy one, and Manifold's `verified` field is true for a bought one.
- Do not buy Premium to rescue posts nobody replies to.

## X in AI answers

- **Grok** answers from X posts in real time (xAI's API exposes an X Search tool beside web search), so an account's own posts and others' posts about the brand are a direct source for Grok's answers about it. Manifold's AI answer tool does not run Grok, so this cannot be measured here.
- **Other engines** rarely surface X: x.com was not among the ten most-cited domains across engines in Peec AI's 30M-source analysis (March 2026), where LinkedIn was third. For visibility in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Google's AI answers, X posts are a weak lever; see [ai-search](../../ai-search/SKILL.md).
- **Unknown:** whether posts on X influence other engines indirectly (through coverage they spark). Nobody has measured it.

## A first 90 days

| Period | Work | Re-measure |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1 to 30 | Audit the account and two competitors; fix the bio and pinned post; pick two or three post shapes from the outliers; founder replies daily in the niche | Baseline scorecard |
| Days 31 to 60 | Post the chosen shapes on a steady weekly cadence; test link and no-link posts on similar topics; Premium if the decision above says so | Views per follower and replies per view against the baseline |
| Days 61 to 90 | Drop the shapes below the median; double the ones above; re-run the audit on the same handles | Same scorecard, same handles |

Promise inputs (posts, replies, a measured test), not follower counts. A model change can reshape reach overnight, as the July 2026 reply boost did; re-check the plan after any change X posts to its algorithm repository.
