# How Instagram ranks content

What Instagram and Adam Mosseri have said about distribution from 2025 to October 2026, and what changed in the product. Most dated coverage here is Social Media Today's reporting of Mosseri's posts and Instagram announcements; Instagram's own pages are cited where they exist. Checked October 2026; Instagram changes something most weeks, so re-check anything older than six months.

## The signals Instagram names

- **Watch time, likes and sends are the top three.** Mosseri, January 2025: creators should watch average watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach. Likes weigh slightly more with followers ("connected" reach), sends slightly more with non-followers ("unconnected" reach). He repeated the split in May 2026. A send is a share by DM; it is the signal most tied to reaching new people.
- **Rates, not totals.** Mosseri, September 2026: judge reach by engagement rate, not total views or likes. Per reach, so a post shown to more people is not credited for volume alone.
- **Four principles he gave in September 2026:** originality (the original creator wins over reposts), recency (the feed runs roughly in chronological order among what it ranks), breaking content (posts from small accounts are shown to some non-followers to test the response), and larger models that weigh more signals.
- **Surveys now steer Reels.** Meta's engineering post of 14 January 2026: short surveys shown between Reels feed the ranking alongside watch time and likes, and raised how well recommendations matched stated interests from 48.3% to over 70%. Watching to the end of something people say they did not want does not help.
- **What Instagram's ranking explainer lists** (last updated May 2023, still the official page): for Reels, the predicted actions are reshares, watching to the end, likes and visits to the audio page; for Explore, likes, saves and shares. Reels that are watermarked, already posted on Instagram, muted, bordered, low-resolution or mostly text are shown less. [Instagram ranking explained](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/instagram-ranking-explained).
- **Insights changed to match.** Views became the single primary metric across formats in August 2025, replacing plays, impressions and accounts reached. Skip rate (viewers who leave in the first 3 seconds) replaced view rate and a retention chart arrived in August 2025; share rate followed in April 2026. Competitive Insights in the Professional Dashboard (November 2025) compares follower gains and posting frequency with up to 10 competitors, free.

## Originality

- **2024 (Reels):** when two reels are identical, Instagram shows the original and labels the copy; accounts that repost others' reels repeatedly (reported as 10 or more times in 30 days) lose recommendations.
- **March 2026 (Facebook and Instagram):** reaction-only, stitched or narrated clips that add nothing are classed as unoriginal and pushed down. Creators get alerts in the Professional Dashboard about reels similar to theirs and about impostor accounts.
- **30 April 2026 (photos and carousels too):** an account is judged on a rolling 30 days. If most of what it posted is unoriginal, it is no longer recommended to non-followers; followers still see it. Original means made by the account or materially changed: added context, graphics with new information, a Remix that transforms the source. A watermark, a crop or a credit in the caption is not enough. Publishers with licences or permission are exempt. Instagram's own routes for sharing others' work are Collabs, Remix, the paid partnership label, Story shares and Reposts. Account Status in settings shows eligibility and takes appeals. Instagram said about three-quarters of US recommendations now come from original posts (MediaPost, May 2026).
- **AI-generated people (September 2026):** profiles that feature an AI-generated person must carry the "AI-generated profile" label; unlabelled ones are not recommended.
- **What this means for a brand:** a feed of reposted creator videos, memes or customer clips "with credit" is an aggregator feed and gets no new reach. Repost through Collab (the creator co-authors), or add real commentary. A UGC programme should run on Collabs or on the creator's own account, not on reuploads.

## Formats and length

- **Reels are recommended up to 3 minutes.** Instagram's Reels guide (September 2026) names 3 seconds to 3 minutes; the camera has recorded up to 20 minutes since November 2025, but Instagram has said repeatedly it recommends only reels under 3 minutes. Longer videos reach mostly followers.
- **Trial Reels** (all professional accounts since December 2024) go to non-followers first; results come after 24 hours, and the reel can then be shared to followers, by hand or automatically on its first 72 hours. Schedulable since April 2026. Instagram said in June 2025 that 40% of creators using them posted more often and their non-follower reach rose 80% (Instagram's own figure, no method given). The right tool for testing a hook, format or topic without the grid paying for a miss.
- **Carousels** stay a strong format for saves and engagement per view (see [benchmarks](benchmarks.md)); slides can be rearranged after posting (March 2026), Instagram is testing a caption per slide (May 2026), and Edits gained carousel editing (October 2026). Instagram tells creators carousels and single images are also judged on originality since April 2026.
- **Reposts** (August 2025): a public reel or post reposted by someone can be recommended to the reposter's followers, credited to the original. Being reposted is reach; reposting is not original content.
- **Collabs:** creator-published collabs with a brand invited performed best in Emplifi's January to June 2026 data (3,791 brand profiles), with more than 5 times the amplification of non-collab posts in ecommerce and retail; reels gained most. The method is not published in detail.
- **The grid is 3:4** since January 2025 (4:5 images are cropped at the sides in the grid view). Tagged posts can be added to the grid (September 2026), the grid can be rearranged (June 2026) and thumbnails edited (March 2026).
- **The app leans to Reels and DMs.** 3 billion monthly users and DMs moved into the bottom bar beside Home and Reels (September 2025); Instagram is testing opening the app on Reels (October 2026). Reels were over half of time spent by Meta's late-2024 figure; no newer number is public.
- **Edits:** Mosseri said in August 2025 that reels made in Edits get a small reach boost "for now", and that running a finished video through Edits to get it does not work.

## Hashtags, captions and search

- **Five hashtags at most** per post and reel since December 2025. Mosseri (February 2025): hashtags do not increase reach; a few specific ones help categorise and help search.
- **Captions are read for search and topic.** Instagram (August 2026): use plain, descriptive keywords, because search and its sorting read the caption; only the first two lines show before "more", so the first line is the caption hook. Caption length does not move reach much either way (Mosseri, August 2025).
- **Google indexes public posts** from adult accounts (photos and reels since 2020, expanding by region from 10 July 2025), and Instagram generates titles and descriptions for those results with AI (December 2025). A caption is now also a search snippet.
- **Debunked by Mosseri:** "link in bio" lowers reach (it does not, July 2025); reposting your own feed post to Stories adds reach (not meaningfully, April 2026); engaging with accounts like yours changes who sees your posts (it does not, August 2025). His line on reach hacks: they "sometimes work, usually don't", and Instagram closes the ones it finds.

## Viewer controls

- **Your Algorithm** lets a viewer see and edit the topics Instagram has assigned them: tested on Reels from October 2025, US in December 2025, all English-speaking users in January 2026, Explore in April 2026 and the main feed in June 2026. Mosseri warned against dropping algorithmic ranking altogether (September 2026), while EU regulators push for non-personalised feeds.
- Inference, not an Instagram statement: when viewers curate by topic, an account whose posts sit clearly in one topic is easier to match than one that changes subject every post.

## Teen accounts

- **13+ by default.** From 14 October 2025 in the US, UK, Australia and Canada, and worldwide from June 2026, under-18s sit in a PG-13-style setting they cannot leave without a parent: less suggestive content, strong language, risky stunts and drug references in Feed, Reels and Explore, and less interaction with accounts that mainly post such content. Teens also see less on topics such as nutrition, weightlifting and coping with anxiety (June 2026). Parents can choose a stricter Limited Content mode that also removes comments.
- **For marketers:** reach among teens cannot be assumed for diet, supplement, fitness, alcohol, dating or edgy humour content, even when it breaks no rule. Australia's under-16 ban (Meta removed over 540,000 suspected teen accounts by January 2026) and New Zealand's restrictions (August 2026) shrink that audience further. Meta also lets advertisers target teens by age and location only (Meta, from February 2023); ads are the [paid-ads](../../paid-ads/SKILL.md) skill's.
