# Ethics and law

Where competitive research stops being research. Not legal advice: when a step is close to a line, tell the user to ask a lawyer in their market. Checked October 2026.

## Gathering

- **Public is fine.** Competitors' websites, pricing pages, ad libraries, public social posts, job ads, press, filings, reviews and public forum posts are fair to read, and the ad libraries exist so the public can read them. In the US, scraping public data while logged out was held not to breach Meta's terms (Meta v. Bright Data, January 2024) or X's (X Corp v. Bright Data, dismissed May 2024). Logged-in scraping against a site's terms is a different matter. Manifold's tools read public data through its vendors; the user needs no account on the platform.
- **Misrepresentation is the line.** Posing as a prospect to get a demo, a trial, a price sheet or a sales deck; using a customer's, contractor's or ex-employee's login; asking a competitor's former staff for confidential material. These are where trade-secret and computer-misuse claims come from. Appian won a $2.036B trade-secret verdict against Pegasystems in May 2022 over Pega's use of a government contractor's employee, who had access, to study Appian's platform; the Court of Appeals of Virginia reversed it in July 2024, and the Supreme Court of Virginia affirmed that and ordered a new trial on 8 January 2026. Even a reversed verdict meant years of litigation. The professional standard (SCIP's code of ethics) is to disclose your identity and organisation before any interview and to comply with all applicable laws.
- **Never** create accounts to get gated competitor content, impersonate anyone, or ask the user to. A rival's public free trial taken in the user's own name and within its terms is the user's decision; flag the terms.
- **Personal data.** Competitive work needs companies, not people. Leave usernames out of research tables, never compile profiles of a competitor's staff, and follow [outbound](../../outbound/SKILL.md) for any personal data.

## Using

- **Comparative claims are legal when true and substantiated.** The FTC has encouraged truthful comparative advertising since August 1979 (its policy statement at 16 CFR 14.15, 44 FR 47328); in the US a false or misleading claim about a competitor is actionable under the Lanham Act. In the EU, comparative advertising must compare like with like objectively and not mislead, discredit or take unfair advantage of a trademark (Directive 2006/114/EC, Article 4); the UK's CAP Code has the same rules (ASA guidance on comparisons with identifiable competitors). So: every claim on a comparison page, ad or battlecard needs evidence the user could produce, dated, on comparable plans.
- **Naming competitors.** Using a rival's name to identify it ("X vs Y", "an alternative to X") is generally allowed in the US and EU; implying endorsement or using its logo as your own branding is not. Bidding on rival brand names is in [paid-ads](../../paid-ads/SKILL.md).
- **Reviews.** The FTC's rule on consumer reviews (16 CFR Part 465, announced August 2024, in force 21 October 2024, civil penalties for knowing violations) bans fake reviews including AI-written ones, buying reviews conditioned on sentiment, undisclosed insider reviews, suppressing negative reviews with threats, presenting a company-controlled site as independent reviews of a category that includes its own products, and buying fake followers or views. Never write, buy or seed reviews of the user or of a competitor, and never present the user's own "best X" page as independent.
- **Replies in public threads.** Disclose the affiliation, answer the question, state the difference, never attack. Never post for the user ([reddit](../../reddit/SKILL.md)).
- **What a rep says.** False statements about a rival's product to a buyer are disparagement wherever they are made, including on a call. Battlecard counters must be true and provable; landmines are questions, not claims ([battlecards](battlecards.md)).

## Quick test

Would the user be comfortable if the competitor read exactly how this was gathered and how it is being used? If not, do not do it.
