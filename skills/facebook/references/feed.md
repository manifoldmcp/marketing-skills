# How the Facebook feed works now

What decides who sees a page's posts in 2026, and the policy changes since mid-2025 that make older advice wrong. Checked October 2026; Meta changes ranking and policy often, so re-check anything here older than six months.

## Where feed views come from

- **Recommendations are over a third of what Americans see.** In Q1 2026, 48.1% of US Feed content views came from friends, groups people had joined or accounts they follow, and 39.0% from in-feed recommendations of content from accounts they do not follow; the rest is "other" (Meta, Widely Viewed Content Report, Q1 2026). A secondary reading of the Q2 2026 report gives 49.6% connected and 36.6% recommended. In Q3 2022 connected sources were 76.5% and recommendations 15.2%, under definitions that have since changed, so the trend is clear but the series is not exact.
- **What follows from that:** a page's posts reach few of its own followers, and most new reach is decided by the recommendation system, which scores the post, not the page's size. A small page can reach far past its followers; a large page has no claim on its own.
- **Followers still matter for links.** The few link posts that are seen are mostly seen by people who follow the page (Meta's report, Q2 2025).
- **The Friends tab** (March 2025, US and Canada first) shows only friends' posts, reels and stories with no recommendations. The Home feed still recommends. Meta has published no usage figures for the tab.

## Video and Reels

- **Video time is where Facebook is growing.** Facebook video time rose 9% globally and over 10% in the US and Canada year on year in Q2 2026, which Meta credited to ranking improvements (Q2 2026 earnings call, 29 July 2026, as summarised by MarketBeat). Meta also said it uses large language models to understand content for ranking, starting on Instagram and extending to more Facebook surfaces.
- **Reels ranking now asks viewers.** Since early 2026, Facebook Reels ranking is trained on in-feed surveys asking how well a video matches the viewer's interests, not on engagement alone. In a test on over 10 million users, low survey ratings fell 6.84% and total engagement rose 5.2% (Engineering at Meta, 14 January 2026). The practical reading, ours not Meta's: a reel that a specific audience says is for them beats a broad, bait-heavy reel.
- **Engagement on reels is not higher than on other formats for pages.** By follower count, 2025 engagement was status 0.20%, album 0.18%, reel 0.18%, image 0.15%, link 0.05% (Socialinsider, 25M posts from 130,683 business pages, January 2024 to December 2025, published March 2026). Reels win on reach to non-followers, not on rate. Socialinsider's own text and tables disagree in places; quote the tables.

## Original content (since July 2025)

- **Reposting others' work is penalised.** From 14 July 2025, accounts that repeatedly reuse other creators' videos, photos or text without permission or meaningful change lose monetisation for a period and get reduced distribution on everything they post; duplicate videos are demoted in favour of the original. Stitching clips together or adding a watermark does not count as a change; commentary, voiceover and creative editing do. In the first half of 2025 Meta actioned about 500,000 accounts for spammy behaviour or fake engagement and removed about 10 million profiles impersonating large creators (Meta, Facebook creators blog, 14 July 2025).
- **Professional Dashboard shows the penalty.** Post-level insights explain why a post is not being distributed, and the Support home screen shows whether a page is at risk of recommendation or monetisation penalties. For the user's own page, ask them to check it before diagnosing a reach drop.
- **For a business page:** a feed of reshared memes, stock reels or other people's clips now hurts the whole page, not only those posts. Post what the business itself made.
- **Engagement bait is still demoted.** Posts that ask for likes, shares, tags or comments as a mechanic ("like and share to win") get reduced distribution under Meta's Content Distribution Guidelines. They still pull inflated numbers from followers: in our test a page's like-share-tag giveaway drew about 25 times its median engagement (see [field notes](field-notes.md)). Leave giveaways out of medians and do not read their engagement as interest.

## Links

- **Links are almost absent from what people see.** 98.9% of US Feed views in Q2 2026 were on posts without a link to a site outside Facebook (Meta's report as quoted in coverage), up from 97.8% in Q2 2025 and 86.5% in Q3 2021.
- **Link posts earn the least.** 0.05% engagement by followers in both 2024 and 2025, the lowest of any format and the fewest views at every page size; pages halved their link posts from 19 to 10 a month (Socialinsider, as above).
- **Link posts are capped for some pages.** From 16 December 2025 Meta tested limiting some Pages and professional-mode profiles without Meta Verified to links in 2 organic posts a month; publishers were excluded and links in comments were not capped (Social Media Today, 17 December 2025; Social Media Examiner, 5 February 2026). Reports from September 2026 say the cap reached more Pages alongside Meta's new business subscription tiers; we could not verify that against a Meta statement. Before a plan leans on link posts, ask the user whether their page shows a link limit.
- Whether a link in the first comment carries reach the way a link in the post does not is untested; no study measures it.

## Creators and monetisation

- Facebook paid creators nearly $3 billion in 2025, up 35%, with about 60% from Reels; creators earning over $10,000 a year grew over 30% (Meta, March 2026). Payouts run through one Content Monetization program, merged from in-stream ads, Reels ads and the performance bonus in October 2024.
- Creator Fast Track (March 2026) pays creators with 100,000+ followers on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube $1,000 a month, and over 1 million up to $3,000, for three months, to start posting on Facebook. Meta added "qualified view" and earnings-rate metrics to show what earns.
- What this means for a business: creators are being paid to post original video on Facebook, so recommended reels are a crowded slot. It also means creators with Facebook audiences are easier to find than in 2024; sizing and vetting them is [creators](../../creators/SKILL.md).

## Paid reach

Boosting and ads are a separate auction and do not change organic distribution as far as anyone has shown. Ads, boosting and Meta Verified's ad-related perks belong to [paid-ads](../../paid-ads/SKILL.md). Whether paying for Meta Verified raises organic reach beyond lifting the link cap is unmeasured.
