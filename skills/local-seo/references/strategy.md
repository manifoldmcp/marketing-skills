# A local SEO plan

When the user wants a plan rather than one check: where the business stands on Maps, in the pack and in AI answers across its town, and the work that closes the gap fastest, over 90 days. Checked October 2026.

## Order of work

The order follows what blocks what. Skip a step only when the baseline shows it is not a problem.

1. **Eligibility.** The profile is claimed, verified, not suspended, open, in the right primary category, at a real address (hidden for a service-area business), with no duplicate in the same category at the same address. Nothing else works while one of these fails ([Business Profile](business-profile.md)).
2. **Relevance on the profile.** Categories, predefined and custom services, hours (including holidays), attributes, description, photos, and the link to the right page. An afternoon's work that the 2026 survey rates highly.
3. **The page the profile links to.** One page per service and, for several locations, one per location, with the town in the title and the services, areas, prices and hours in plain text. This feeds the pack, local organic results and AI answers alike ([seo](../../seo/SKILL.md) for the page work).
4. **Reviews.** A steady ask to every customer and a reply to every review, within Google's policy and the FTC and UK rules ([reviews](reviews.md)).
5. **Prominence beyond Google.** Yelp and the trade's main directory claimed and consistent; inclusion on the local best-of lists and press the AI engines cite ([AI answers](ai-answers.md), [digital-pr](../../digital-pr/SKILL.md)); local links ([link-building](../../link-building/SKILL.md)).
6. **Spam above the user.** Where a fake or stuffed listing holds the pack, prepare the evidence for the owner to report ([competitors](competitors.md#spam-in-the-pack)).

Choose by the grid's pattern: weak even at the address means steps 1 to 3; strong at the address and fading means steps 4 and 5 (prominence to push the ring out) or a real second location; one rival everywhere means study that rival first.

## Baseline

- A [rank grid](rank-grid.md) for two or three searches (5 by 5, 25 credits each), plus `seo_get_serp` for the same searches at the town and two ZIP or postcodes (1 credit each).
- `maps_get_place` on the business and the three competitors that recur in the grid (8 credits), filled into the [competitors](competitors.md) table.
- `aeo_run_ai_answers` on four or five local prompts on chatgpt, gemini, ai_overview and ai_mode (8 credits a prompt).
- About 105 credits for two searches, 130 for three. Store every number with the date and the points in the host; the server keeps no history.

## The 90 days

| Period | Typical work | KPI the tools re-measure |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1 to 30 | Eligibility fixed, profile completed (categories, services, hours, attributes, photos, description), linked page rebuilt, review ask and reply routine started, spam evidence prepared | Audit rows passing; grid top-3 share at the address |
| Days 31 to 60 | Review flow at or above the leading rival's rate, Yelp and the trade directory cleaned up, service and area pages on the site, first best-of lists and local press approached | New reviews a month against rivals; grid top-3 and top-10 share |
| Days 61 to 90 | Second round of lists and links, refresh the profile (photos, services, offers), re-run the full baseline | Grid shares and average rank against the baseline; AI mentions across prompts and engines |

Give each period an owner, and three first actions for this week.

## Setting targets honestly

- **Promise inputs, not positions.** No one can promise the pack: proximity is fixed, and Google says rank cannot be bought. Promise a complete profile, a review flow, the pages, and the listings and lists.
- **Expect the ring to grow, not the city to fall.** A realistic 90-day target is a higher top-3 share around the address and in the next ring out, not the top 3 everywhere in town.
- **Profile edits can show within weeks; reviews and prominence take months.** A category change that is right can move the grid within two to four weeks; review counts compound slowly. No controlled study gives timings: re-measure, do not predict.
- **Mind what triggers re-verification or suspension** before any edit to the name, category or address, and never take a shortcut (a keyword in the name, a virtual office, a review contest) that puts the profile at risk. A suspended profile loses everything at once.
- **AI answers name few businesses.** Report a mention rate across prompts and engines with its noise ([ai-search](../../ai-search/SKILL.md)), never a "rank in ChatGPT".
