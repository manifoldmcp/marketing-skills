# Apple Ads and organic

How paid App Store search interacts with organic ranking and installs. Running and bidding a campaign is a paid-ads job, but the store side changed enough in 2026 to sit here. Checked October 2026.

## What changed

- **More ad slots in search results.** Apple announced in December 2025 that ads would appear further down App Store search results, not only in the single top slot; the rollout started on 3 March 2026 in the UK and Japan and reached the US and other markets by the end of March, on iOS 26.2 and later. Advertisers cannot pick a position; existing campaigns became eligible automatically; billing stays cost per tap or per install. Apple has not said how many slots there are.
- **Apple says relevance gates the auction:** an app not relevant to the search does not show "regardless of how much you may be willing to pay".
- Search Ads was renamed Apple Ads in April 2025; from March 2026 "Maximize Conversions" bidding replaced the manual CPA cap, and in August 2026 Apple Ads came to Apple Maps in the US and Canada (AppTweak news log).

## Effect on organic

- **Organic first place now sits lower on the screen** on searches with several ads, so the same organic rank is worth fewer taps than in 2025. No independent study has measured by how much. A developer reported organic installs falling while paid rose and total installs stayed flat after the March rollout (one anecdote); agency projections of a 10% to 15% organic drop have no published method.
- **App Store Connect counts Apple Ads installs inside the App Store search source**, so a rise in paid can hide a fall in organic. Split them with Apple Ads' own reporting before reading organic search trends.
- **The halo claim is unproven.** Vendors say paid installs lift organic rank, because installs feed ranking. The mechanism is plausible, but no independent controlled study has published a size.
- **Brand bidding:** if competitors bid on the user's brand, the top of the user's own brand search can go to a rival. Whether the user's own brand campaign is incremental is answered only by an on/off or geo holdout test of at least two weeks per period, comparing total installs (paid plus organic), not paid installs alone. Without a test, report the brand campaign's installs as partly ones the user would have had anyway.

## Using Apple Ads as research

- **Search Popularity (5 to 100)** in Apple Ads is the best iOS demand signal, free with an account ([keywords](keywords.md)).
- **Search term reports** from a discovery campaign show the real queries that led to taps and installs, with tap-through and conversion per term: the closest thing to query logs for the App Store. A term that converts well on paid is a strong organic target.
- **Custom product pages** can be ad variations as well as organic pages; a vendor analysis of over a million Apple Ads ad groups reported higher tap-through and conversion with them (vendor data on paid traffic, no method published).

## Manifold
