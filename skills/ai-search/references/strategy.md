# An AI search plan

When the user wants a plan rather than a check: which buyer questions to win, where the brand stands on each engine, and which work closes the gap fastest, over 90 days.

## Order of work

The order follows what blocks what. Skip a step only when the baseline shows it is not a problem.

1. **Access.** Any `blocks_citations` failure from `aeo_get_site_readiness` comes first, because nothing else works while engines cannot read the site ([site access](site-access.md)).
2. **Wrong facts.** An answer that gets pricing or capability wrong costs sales now ([wrong answers](wrong-answers.md)).
3. **Third-party sources.** The cited pages that name competitors and not the user, ranked by engines and prompts citing them ([citations](citations.md)). This is usually the bulk of the work and the biggest lever, because mentions follow being talked about elsewhere.
4. **Owned pages.** Pages that answer the buyer questions directly and are kept current: pricing, comparison (honest, not self-ranking), use-case and docs pages. Substantive updates to existing pages beat new pages ([citations](citations.md#freshness)).
5. **Google.** Where AI Overviews or AI Mode matter, measure them per keyword: ranking helps but no longer predicts citation ([Google's AI answers](google-ai-overviews.md)).

Choose per engine. A B2B brand losing on ChatGPT needs off-site mentions and editorial coverage; a local business losing on Gemini needs its own site and listings; a shop on ChatGPT needs its product feed.

## Baseline

A fixed prompt set of ten to forty buyer prompts on the engines the buyers use, scored as [measurement](measurement.md) says, with the margin of error beside each share. About 270 credits with Manifold for ten prompts on five engines plus the prompt research; 180 for each re-measure. Readiness is free.

## The 90 days

| Period | Typical work | KPI the tools re-measure |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1 to 30 | Access fixed, wrong facts corrected, first 15 cited sources contacted, owned answer pages updated | Readiness checks passing; wrong claims gone on re-run |
| Days 31 to 60 | List and editorial inclusions, Reddit and YouTube participation where those engines cite them, new or rebuilt answer pages | Cited sources now naming the user |
| Days 61 to 90 | Second round of sources, re-measure on the same prompt set | Mention share and citation share per engine, against the margin |

Give each period an owner, and three first actions for this week.

## Setting targets honestly

- Promise inputs, not positions: a readable site, correct facts, presence on the sources engines cite. No one can promise a place in an answer, and lists rarely repeat ([measurement](measurement.md#noise)).
- Set KPI targets larger than the margin of error on the set's size. On 50 answers a ten-point move may be noise.
- Engines that search live can move in weeks; answers from training data move on model releases. Say which is which when setting the 30-day target, and say that no study has measured how long a change takes to show.
- A model release can reshape an engine overnight (GPT-5.6 cut listicle and Reddit citations in August 2026). Re-check the plan's assumptions after any major release.
